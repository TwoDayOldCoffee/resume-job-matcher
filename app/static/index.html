How it works

  1. ReadAn LLM turns your resume into a structured profile: role, seniority, skills and domains.
  2. SearchSemantic search finds the 20 closest postings, adjusted so roles match your seniority.
  3. RankAn LLM reorders those 20 by real fit and keeps the best 10.
  4. ExplainFor the top 5, it lists why each fits and which stated requirements you don't yet show.
Built with bge-small embeddings, gpt-oss on Groq and FastAPI. Job postings come from a public LinkedIn dataset (2023-24) and are no longer live.
